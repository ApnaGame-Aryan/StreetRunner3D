Street Runner 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any account. We do not run servers that collect your personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, coins, settings and high scores on your own device (browser or app storage). This data stays on your device and is removed if you clear site data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The game may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings. Learn more: How Google uses information.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Third-party libraries

The game loads its 3D graphics library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). Your IP address is visible to that provider when the library loads.

Children

This game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
